Problem 1: the parser reads day-first dates month-first (379 reports)

Every tenant on file writes the day before the month. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a booking for 7 April lands on 4 July. This is the known problem, and it is most of the ticket: 379 of 412 reports, all 11 tenants, every channel, 1 April to 4 October.

Intake did not see it cleanly. Intake tags each report from the reporter's browser, and 23 of these 379 arrived tagged as the other problem.

Problem 2: the booking-v3 form hint teaches the wrong order (33 reports)

Three tenants (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour) run booking-v3, whose date field is labelled month-first. Their people type what the label says, and the form then reads the date the tenant's way, day first. The typed date flips before the parser ever sees it.

This is a different bug with a different fix: correct the label (or the form's reading of it), not the parser. It covers 33 reports, all through the form, 17 April to 22 September: Alder Physio 12, Copper Studio 11, Pale Harbour 10. Until now it had no ticket of its own; the table above has been showing it as "not this ticket" while the ticket text said one problem.

Filed

  • One GitHub issue for the parser problem, covering 379 reports.
  • One GitHub issue for the hint problem, covering 33 reports.

Outcome

The desk has since closed #4471 (5 October), and the two problems ended differently:

  • The parser problem is fixed: dates are now parsed day/month for EU locales. Its ticket is closed as completed.
  • The hint problem is marked won't fix: booking-v3 is retired, so the form that taught the wrong order is gone. Its ticket is closed as not planned.

This page is closed to match: the split it records is the split the desk acted on.

Lessons

  • A merged ticket can be marked known and still hide a second problem. The count on the ticket is not the shape of the ticket.
  • Intake tags are a guess made from a browser. The cause belongs to the tenant: their locale and their form. 38 of 412 reports were tagged as the other cause.
  • Five reports in the parser group read, in their text, as bookings moved by one day. They are counted under the parser, where their tenants put them, and footnoted by the table.
